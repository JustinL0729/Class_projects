History:

My dog's name is Mina, she's a girl and I have had her for almost 8 years. Shes aggressive towards other strangers but can easily get used to people, it may take a lot of barking but only about 20 minutes. Originally she was supposed to be my mother's dog but she ended up loving me more so then she became my dog. She has been my support animal for a long time and I don't know what I would do without her. Her birthday is in october, somewhere near halloween which I find ironic as she seems pretty scary.

About:

She is a purebred, black chihuahua with a little bit of white on her paws and chest. Shes 7 inches tall, 5 pounds and smooth-haired. When shes outside she is very energetic but then when inside she seems tired and sleeps a lot. When you give her a treat she will jump about twice her height as she really wants the treat. She likes to follow me around a lot especially when I have food. When she sees any human food, she will immediately switch up on you and just stare at the food instead.
