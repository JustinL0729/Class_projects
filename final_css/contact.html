Pros:

  • Their small size helps them be portable and perfect for apartments or other small places.
  • They have a long lifespan known to average around 14 to 18 years.
  • Chihuahuas are very loyal and affectionate.
  • They dont need much grooming as they have a small coat of fur that they usually maintain.
  • Chihuahuas are a very intellectual type of dog even when they are quite aggressive towards strangers.
  • Even with their aggression, they are alert and protective of their owner. They do not let their size get in the way of their protection.

Cons:

  • They are very fragile as it comes with their size, any big fall or bad owner can hurt them easily.
  • They have a higher chance to have dental issues, this tends to give them bad breath.
  • Along their are also prone to heart issues and leg issues like Patellar Luxation.
  • They bark a lot without being trained, usually at anything that passes by them.
  • Some are not always patient with young children and need socialization themselves to avoid aggression in the future.
  • With training, it takes a long time and consistant training to get the chihuahua to behave well in public.
