Possessive:

They are very affectionate, even through all their sassy attitudes, they are happy and comfortable with their owners. This can also transfer to their toys, they will growl at anyone who poses as a threat to their belongings. A way that they show that this is their land is by leaving their manure on the grass as marking their territory.

Short-Tempered:

From experience I can agree that chihuahuas get angry very easily, seeing a stranger or another dog is enought to tilt them off. Some may say they are "a big dog within a small dog body" from their mentality. One thing I like to say is how they are all bark no bite, which is mostly true as since they aren't the biggest dog so they can't do much fighting. This leads to chihuahuas being loud to eachother but nothing physical.

Attention-Seeking:

They love attention especially from their owner, a good way to train them is consistant socialization in public to get them used to others. They dont like being left alone for long periods of time, I can relate to this as my dog is very excited when I return home to her as if its been years since I last saw her. I can't speak for all chihuahuas, but some may have an "attachment disorder" where they are scared to be away from their owner.
